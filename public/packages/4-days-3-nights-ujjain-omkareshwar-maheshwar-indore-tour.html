Detailed Schedule

Day-by-Day Sacred Itinerary

100% Fully Customizable
1
Day 1 Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Divine Ujjain Mahakal & Sacred Shrines Circuit

🏨 Super Deluxe Hotel, Ujjain

Morning pickup from Indore or Ujjain. Check-in at hotel and proceed to Shree Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga for sacred VIP Darshan and Mahakal Lok exploration. Visit Sandipani Ashram, Kaal Bhairav, Gadhkalika, Bada Ganesh, and Bharthari Gufa. In the evening, witness the grand Shipra Aarti at Ram Ghat and seek blessings at Harsiddhi Mata Mandir before delicious dinner.

2
Day 2 Detailed Itinerary

Day 2: Sacred Omkareshwar & Mamleshwar Jyotirlinga Pilgrimage

🏨 Super Deluxe Hotel, Maheshwar / Omkareshwar

Post breakfast, drive to Omkareshwar on the sacred Narmada. Board a boat to the island Jyotirlinga. Experience VIP Darshan at Omkareshwar and Mamleshwar Mahadev. After peaceful prayers and lunch, visit Shani Mandir and proceed to Maheshwar for night stay.

3
Day 3 Detailed Itinerary

Day 3: Royal Maheshwar Fort, Ahilya Ghat & Sahastradhara

🏨 Super Deluxe Hotel, Indore

Morning tour of magnificent Ahilya Fort on the banks of Narmada. Visit Ahilyeshwar Temple, Ahilya Ghat, and enjoy a river boat ride. Marvel at Sahastradhara rocky waterfalls and witness royal handloom weaving at Rehwa Society. Drive across scenic Jam Gate pass to Indore for night stay.

4
Day 4 Detailed Itinerary

Day 4: Indore City Heritage, Sacred Temples & Food Paradise

🏨 Tour concludes with evening drop at Indore Airport / Station

After breakfast, start your spiritual and royal tour of Indore: pray at Ranjeet Hanuman Mandir, Annapurna Mata Mandir, and Bada Ganesh / Khajrana Ganesh. Marvel at the intricate Belgian glass work at Kanch Mandir, tour the royal Durbar Hall of Lal Bagh Palace, and visit the historic 7-story Rajwada Palace. Indulge in culinary treats at the famous 56 Dukan (Chappan Dukan) and shop at Rajwada Market before drop-off at Indore Airport/Station.
